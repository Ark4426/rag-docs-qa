# Helios Rooftop Solar – Installer Handbook

## Panels
Each Helios HX-410 panel is rated at 410 watts peak and weighs 21.5 kg. Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty guaranteeing at least 84.8% of rated output at year 25.

## Inverter
The HX system uses the Volta S6 string inverter, rated at 6 kW with a peak efficiency of 97.6%. The inverter must be installed in shade with at least 30 cm clearance on every side.

## Maintenance
Panels should be cleaned twice a year with deionised water. Never use pressure washers, as they void the warranty. Inverter firmware is updated over Wi-Fi every quarter.

## Safety
Installers must isolate the DC isolator before opening the inverter. Roof work above 2 metres requires a harness anchored to a certified point.
