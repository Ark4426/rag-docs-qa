# Northwind Ltd – Employee Leave Policy (2026)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 26 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees may self-certify sickness for up to 7 calendar days. Absences longer than 7 days require a doctor's note. Sick pay is 100% of salary for the first 20 working days in any rolling 12-month period.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Partners receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the first year.

## Remote work
Staff may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Working from abroad is limited to 20 days per year.
